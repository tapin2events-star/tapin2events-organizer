Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

This policy explains what information TapIN LLC ("TapIN," "we," "us") collects when you use the TapIN2Events mobile app and website (together, the "Service"), how we use and share it, and the choices you have. TapIN LLC is based in Rolesville, North Carolina, USA.

The short version: we don't sell your personal information and we don't show ads. We use a small number of service providers to run the Service, and limited app analytics to understand how the app is used and how it performs.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, and, if you add them, your phone number, city and state, bio, and profile photo.
  • Organizer, resource, and seller details: events you create or import, resource profiles, products, photos, music and video links, pricing, lineups and schedules, and vendor applications.
  • Posts and activity: videos, captions, comments, likes, follows, tags, saved events, interests, reports, and tips.
  • Messages: booking requests, booking messages, and notes you send to organizers or resources.
  • Purchases: tickets, registrations, bookings, orders, and shipping addresses you enter. Card payments are handled by Stripe; TapIN never receives or stores your full card number.
  • Payout details: if you get paid through TapIN, you provide identity and bank details directly to Stripe. We receive your Stripe account status, not your bank account number.
  • Support requests: anything you send us when you contact us.

Information collected as you use TapIN

  • Tickets and check-ins: tickets you hold and when they are scanned at an event.
  • Location, only if you allow it: if you tap "use my location," your device's location is used on your device to center the map and sort nearby results. It isn't saved to your account. City searches are looked up with OpenStreetMap.
  • App usage and diagnostics: the mobile app records basic usage information, such as sessions, active users, device type, operating system, and app version, through our mobile app platform (Twinr). We use this only to understand usage and keep the app working well. We do not use your device's advertising ID.
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, your device receives a token from Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) so we can send you alerts like ticket confirmations, booking updates, and event reminders. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.
  • Device storage: we store your sign-in session and a few preferences on your device so the Service works. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

How we use information

  • To run the Service: show events, process tickets, bookings and orders, pay organizers and resources, check people in, and send confirmations, reminders, and alerts.
  • To personalize Discover, such as "Picked for you," based on your interests and the events you save or attend.
  • To power optional AI tools for organizers, such as drafting event descriptions or messages. Text you enter into these tools is sent to our AI provider to generate a draft.
  • To keep TapIN safe: prevent fraud, enforce our Terms, and review reports.
  • To provide support and improve the Service.
  • To comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

What others can see

Your public profile, posts, comments, events you organize, and resource profiles are visible to other users and visitors. When you buy a ticket or book a resource, the organizer or resource sees the details needed to serve you, such as your name, email, and ticket or booking information. You can make your follow lists private in your profile settings.

We may also share information if required by law, to protect the safety and rights of our users or the public, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy will continue to apply. We never sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal, tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention reasons (such as payment and transaction records), which we keep only as long as required.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and update: view and edit your information in your Profile at any time.
  • Delete your account: see how to delete your account and data, or email us at the address below.
  • Notifications: turn push notifications off in your device settings.
  • Location: location access is optional and can be turned off in your device settings.
  • Copy of your data: email us to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. We will honor those requests as required.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls on our database, and by keeping payment and bank details with Stripe rather than on our systems. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if required by law.

Children

TapIN is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. We will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app or by email.
