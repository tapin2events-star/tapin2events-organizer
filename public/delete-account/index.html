Delete your TapIN2Events account

TapIN2Events is operated by TapIN LLC. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information connected to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the email address you use to sign in to TapIN2Events. This lets us confirm the account is yours.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account." You don't need to give a reason.
  3. We'll confirm we received your request, and we'll email you again when your account has been deleted.

Send your request to:

support@tapin2events.com

Email a deletion request

We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your account, sign-in details, and profile (name, email, phone number, city and state, bio, and photo)
  • Your posts, videos, comments, likes, follows, tags, and saved events
  • Your resource profile, portfolio, and products, if you have them
  • Your booking messages, interests, and preferences
  • Your push notification token

What we keep, and for how long

Some records have to be kept for legal, tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention reasons, even after your account is deleted:

  • Payment and transaction records for tickets, bookings, orders, refunds, and payouts. We keep these for up to 7 years, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Records tied to open issues, such as an unresolved refund, dispute, or safety report, until the issue is resolved.

These records are kept only for those purposes and are not used for anything else. Payment card and bank details are held by Stripe, not TapIN; Stripe keeps its own records under its privacy policy.

Before you delete

  • Tickets for upcoming events will stop working once your account is deleted.
  • If you're an organizer or resource, make sure any pending payouts have been sent and upcoming events or bookings are handled first.
  • Deletion is permanent. If you want to use TapIN again later, you'll need to create a new account.
